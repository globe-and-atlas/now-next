Now / Next is a glanceable Pebble Time 2 watchface with a large clock and one useful detail at a time. It changes that detail across four configurable daily periods: morning, daytime, evening, and night. Set each period's start hour and choose rain chance, current temperature, today's sunset, or watch battery. The footer names the next detail and when it begins.

Choose background, clock digit, and accent colors independently. Show no date or choose YYYY-MM-DD, MM/DD/YYYY, or DD/MM/YYYY. The clock follows your watch's 12/24-hour setting.

Weather comes from Open-Meteo using the connected phone's location, refreshes every 30 minutes, and appears as UNAVAILABLE when data is missing or stale. Temperature is shown in Fahrenheit. Compatible with Pebble Time 2 (Emery).
